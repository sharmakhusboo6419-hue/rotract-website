Rotaract Club of Presidency College

A 21-year legacy of service, leadership, fellowship, and professional growth

RACPC is an institutional Rotaract club under Rotary International’s District 3192, working through service projects, awareness campaigns, and youth leadership development in Bengaluru.

About the Club

The Rotaract Club of Presidency College is an institutional club affiliated with Rotary International through Rotaract District 3192, Zone Arnava. It follows the motto of Service Above Self.

College

The club functions under Presidency College, Bengaluru, where academic excellence, leadership, and holistic student development support Rotaract’s mission.

Mission

To inspire young leaders to create sustainable and meaningful change through service, leadership, fellowship, and professional development.

Club History & Leadership

Founded over 21 years ago, RACPC has grown into one of the prominent institutional clubs in Rotaract District 3192.

The club is led annually by a Board of Directors that typically includes the President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, directors for service avenues, membership, public relations, and other key roles.

That leadership structure keeps the club focused on project execution, member development, and continuity.

Flagship Projects

SWARA

Menstrual hygiene awareness and support through education, outreach, and sanitary kit distribution.

SAHARA

Anti-drug awareness initiative focused on youth education and responsible decision-making.

ASHRAYA

Compassion-driven visits to old age homes, orphanages, and shelter homes to spread happiness.

TECHNOSHALA

Digital literacy initiative for government school students covering technology basics and safety.

Membership & Growth

RACPC welcomes students interested in community service, leadership, volunteering, teamwork, public speaking, event management, and networking. Members gain opportunities to lead projects, attend district events, and build lasting friendships.
